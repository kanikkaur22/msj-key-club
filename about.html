Member recognition program

Let your service be recognized.

The Member Recognition Program, or MRP, recognizes members across the CNH District for their dedication to Key Club.

01

What is it?

Members work toward one of four recognition levels—bronze, silver, gold, or platinum. Each level requires a set number of service hours and additional fulfillments.

02

Why participate?

MRP celebrates your passion and commitment to service. Qualifying members receive an official CNH District award and are invited to walk across the stage at District Convention.

03

How do I learn more?

Review the official CNH recognition requirements, then check with the club Secretary to make sure the service hours on your Member Recognition Form are accurate.

Ready to work toward an award?

Questions about recorded hours may be directed to the Club Secretary.
